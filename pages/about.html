About us

20 years. One specialisation. Hundreds of Australian businesses funded.

GrantReady was founded on a simple observation: most Australian businesses doing genuine R&D are either not claiming or significantly underclaiming. We exist to fix that.

Our mission

Helping Australian innovation get the recognition it deserves.

The R&D Tax Incentive is one of the most generous government programs in the OECD. It returns up to 43.5 cents for every dollar spent on eligible research and development, as a direct cash refund for eligible small companies in a tax loss position. Yet most businesses doing genuine R&D don't access it properly.

Some don't know they qualify. Others have been told by generalist accountants that their activities don't count. Many more are claiming, but claiming significantly less than they are entitled to, and some are overclaiming without realising it.

GrantReady exists to close that gap. As a registered tax agent (solely for the purposes of providing R&D tax advice) with specialist expertise in R&D Tax Incentive and EMDG, we manage the entire claim process on behalf of our clients and deliver outcomes that generalists simply can't match.
