How a referral works

Four steps from introduction to outcome.

  1. You make the introduction

    A brief email or phone call to your client is all it takes. We can provide you with a one-page summary to share with clients you think may be eligible.

  2. We conduct a free eligibility assessment

    GrantReady assesses your client's eligibility and provides them with an honest estimate of potential benefit, at no charge. If they don't qualify, we tell them clearly.

  3. We manage the full claim

    From documentation through AusIndustry registration, we prepare the ATO R&D schedule for you to lodge with your client's tax return, and manage any review process. Your client gets their refund. You retain the relationship.

  4. You stay informed

    We provide claim status updates to you and your client throughout the process, and share the outcome on completion. No surprises.

Common questions

Questions from accounting firms.

Will GrantReady compete with us for the client's tax work?

No. GrantReady is not a general accounting or tax practice. We do not hold an accounting firm licence and we do not offer accounting, audit, or tax compliance services. Our Registered Tax Agent status covers R&D Tax Incentive advice only: we prepare the ATO R&D schedule, but your firm lodges the client's tax return.

How does GrantReady charge?

GrantReady charges a commencement fee plus a performance component tied to the outcome. There is no fee for the initial eligibility assessment. We can provide you with our full fee schedule on request.

Can we be involved in the process?

Yes. Many referring firms choose to be kept across the engagement. We can copy you on key communications and provide a summary of the final claim outcome. The level of involvement is up to you and your client.

What if my client has already been lodging their own R&D claim?

This is one of our most common referral scenarios. Self-lodging clients typically miss 20-40% of eligible activity. GrantReady reviews the prior claim, identifies what was missed, and prepares an improved claim. The uplift is often significant. Ask us about our Health Check.

What do you need from us to assess a client?

A basic introduction is enough to start. For the eligibility assessment, we need to speak with someone at the client business who understands what they are building or developing. We do not need financial statements at the assessment stage.
