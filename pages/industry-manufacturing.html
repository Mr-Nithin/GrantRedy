Eligible activities

What activities typically qualify?

  • New product design and engineering development
  • Materials science and composite materials research
  • Manufacturing process improvement with uncertain outcomes
  • Industrial automation and robotics R&D
  • Quality control system development and testing
  • Tooling and mould design experimentation
  • Energy efficiency and emissions reduction R&D
  • Supply chain technology and logistics system R&D
Eligible expenditure

What costs can be claimed?

  • Engineer and technician wages attributable to R&D
  • Materials and components used in R&D prototypes
  • Equipment costs attributable to R&D activities
  • Testing and certification laboratory costs
  • External engineering consultant costs
  • Software development for manufacturing control systems

FAQs

Common questions for Manufacturing

We improve our production process. Does this qualify?

Process improvement can qualify if it involves genuine experimental activity (testing different approaches, measuring outcomes, and iterating) where the optimal outcome cannot be predicted in advance using existing knowledge.

We produce custom-engineered products for clients. Does this qualify?

R&D conducted primarily to produce a product to a customer specification generally does not qualify. But where your engineers must solve genuinely novel technical problems to achieve the required outcome, those specific activities may qualify.

Can we claim capital equipment?

Generally no, capital equipment costs are not eligible. However, depreciation of equipment attributable to R&D activities may be included. GrantReady can advise on the correct treatment.
