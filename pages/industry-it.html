💻R&D Tax Incentive for IT & Software Companies

Software is one of the most misunderstood sectors under the R&D Tax Incentive. Many software companies assume their work does not qualify, but significant development activity often does, particularly where the technical outcome is uncertain and the work extends beyond applying known methods. Eligible activities

What activities typically qualify?

  • Machine learning model architecture research and training
  • Development of novel algorithms with uncertain performance outcomes
  • New programming language compiler or runtime development
  • Security research and cryptographic system development
  • Natural language processing and computer vision R&D
  • Distributed systems and database architecture experimentation
  • Novel API and integration protocol development
  • Autonomous systems and robotics software development
Eligible expenditure

What costs can be claimed?

  • Developer and data scientist wages attributable to R&D
  • Cloud computing costs for R&D workloads
  • Software tools and licences used for R&D
  • Contractor costs for R&D activities
  • Hardware for R&D testing environments

FAQs

Common questions for IT & Software

Does building a SaaS product qualify?

Not all of it. Routine software development using known frameworks does not qualify. But activities involving genuine technical uncertainty (where the approach, architecture, or algorithm behaviour cannot be determined in advance) often do.

We use off-the-shelf ML frameworks like TensorFlow. Does that disqualify us?

Not necessarily. Using established frameworks as a starting point does not disqualify you if your work involves experimental extension of those frameworks, novel training approaches, or genuinely uncertain performance outcomes.

Our engineers spend time on both eligible and non-eligible work. How do we handle this?

You can claim the portion of wages attributable to R&D activities. GrantReady can help you implement a time-tracking and allocation methodology that meets ATO documentation requirements.
