Eligible activities

What activities typically qualify?

  • Drug discovery and lead compound identification
  • Pre-clinical research and animal model studies
  • Clinical trial design, conduct, and analysis
  • Biologics and biosimilar development
  • Genomics, proteomics, and bioinformatics research
  • Synthetic biology and gene therapy experimentation
  • Process development for manufacturing scale-up
  • Toxicology studies and safety assessment
Eligible expenditure

What costs can be claimed?

  • Wages and salaries of R&D staff (scientists, lab technicians)
  • Contract research organisation (CRO) costs
  • Laboratory consumables and reagents
  • Equipment and facility costs attributable to R&D
  • Regulatory and clinical study costs
  • Intellectual property registration costs

FAQs

Common questions for Biotech

Do Phase 1 and Phase 2 clinical trials qualify?

Yes. Clinical trials (including Phase 1 safety studies, Phase 2 efficacy studies, and early Phase 3 work) generally qualify as core R&D activities because their outcomes are genuinely uncertain and they follow the scientific method.

Can we claim costs paid to a CRO?

Yes. Payments to a contract research organisation (also referred to as a Research Service Provider, or RSP, once registered) for work conducted on your behalf, where you own the results, are generally eligible as contracted R&D expenditure.

We are pre-revenue. Can we still claim?

Absolutely. Pre-revenue companies in a tax loss position actually benefit most from the R&D Tax Incentive: they can receive a direct cash refund of up to 43.5% of eligible spend (indicative), even with zero revenue.
