🌾R&D Tax Incentive for Agriculture & Agtech

Agriculture and food technology companies conducting experimental activities in ag tech, crop science, precision agriculture, aquaculture, or food processing are frequently eligible for significant R&D Tax Incentive claims. Combined with potential EMDG entitlements for export-focused agri-businesses, GrantReady can often identify multiple funding streams simultaneously.

Eligible activities

What activities typically qualify?

  • Ag tech: autonomous farm equipment, spray-weeding drones, and robotic mowers
  • Crop improvement and plant breeding experiments
  • Soil science and nutrient management research
  • Precision agriculture technology development
  • Irrigation and water management systems R&D
  • Livestock genetics and animal husbandry research
  • Food processing and fermentation development
  • Aquaculture system and feed development
Eligible expenditure

What costs can be claimed?

  • Farm staff wages attributable to R&D trials
  • Seeds, inputs, and materials used in experimental plots
  • Equipment attributable to R&D activities
  • Laboratory testing and analysis costs
  • Agronomist and specialist contractor costs
  • Data collection and sensor technology costs

FAQs

Common questions for Agriculture & Agtech

Do on-farm trials qualify as R&D?

Yes, if the trials are designed to test a specific hypothesis with an uncertain outcome, follow a systematic experimental approach, and seek to generate new knowledge beyond existing agricultural practice.

We export our produce. Can we also claim EMDG?

Yes. Agricultural exporters are frequently eligible for the EMDG as well as the R&D Tax Incentive. GrantReady can manage both claims simultaneously and maximise your total government funding recovery.

We're a family farm. Do we qualify?

The R&D Tax Incentive is generally available to incorporated companies (Pty Ltd or Ltd). Sole traders, partnerships, and family trusts cannot usually claim directly, but there are limited exceptions and a corporate structure may be able to access the program.
