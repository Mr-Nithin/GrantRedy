Medical device and biomedical technology companies are among the most consistent beneficiaries of the R&D Tax Incentive. Eligible activities

What activities typically qualify?

  • Medical device design and prototype development
  • Biocompatibility testing and material science research
  • In vitro and in vivo testing of device performance
  • Clinical evaluation and human factors studies
  • TGA/FDA regulatory study design and conduct
  • Software verification and validation for medical software (SaMD)
  • Sterilisation and manufacturing process development
  • Implant and wearable device R&D
Eligible expenditure

What costs can be claimed?

  • Engineering and R&D staff wages
  • Prototype and tooling costs attributable to R&D
  • Testing laboratory and certification costs
  • CRO and clinical evaluation costs
  • IP registration and patent costs
  • Software development costs for device software

FAQs

Common questions for Biomedical

Does TGA regulatory work qualify?

Work conducted to determine whether a device design meets regulatory requirements (where the outcome is not yet known) generally qualifies as a supporting R&D activity.

We manufacture devices under contract. Does this qualify?

Contract manufacturing alone does not qualify. However, process development, material science research, and testing activities conducted to develop the manufacturing process may qualify.

Can software developed for a medical device qualify?

Yes. Software as a Medical Device (SaMD) development, verification, and validation activities generally qualify as R&D, particularly where the software behaviour under certain conditions is uncertain.
