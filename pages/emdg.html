Export Market Development Grant

Recover up to 50% of your overseas marketing expenditure.

The Export Market Development Grant (EMDG) is an Australian Government program administered by Austrade that reimburses up to 50% of eligible export promotional expenditure for businesses with annual income under $20M.

Eligible expenditure

What qualifies for EMDG.

EMDG covers a wide range of promotional activities that have the purpose of developing export sales. Here are the key eligible categories.

🌐

Overseas representation and agents

Fees paid to maintain a representative, distributor, or agent in a foreign country to promote your product or service in that export market.

✈️

Overseas and domestic travel

Travel costs for promotional trips to a foreign country, and trips within Australia specifically to meet with and engage foreign buyers.

🛬

Hosting foreign buyer visits

Costs of bringing potential overseas buyers to Australia to visit your business and see your product firsthand.

📊

Consultants

Fees paid to a consultant engaged to carry out export market research or promotional activities on your behalf.

📣

Soliciting for business overseas

Costs of activities carried out in a foreign country to seek export business, including trade shows, exhibitions, and direct in-market promotion.

🎁

Free samples and promotional items

Product samples provided free of charge to potential foreign buyers to demonstrate your product ahead of an export sale.

📋

Promotional literature and advertising

Production of marketing materials, digital advertising, and brand activity specifically targeting overseas markets.

⚖️

Intellectual property rights

Costs of registering and protecting patents, trademarks, or designs in your export markets.

🎓

Export training

Approved export training if you're a Tier 1 applicant, or training delivered through an approved representative body.

Eligibility

Do you qualify for EMDG?

  • Annual income under $20 million
  • Spent at least $20,000 on eligible export promotional activities
  • Products or services of Australian origin
  • Have an ABN
  • Not a government body or authority
