Textile, Clothing & Footwear

Globally patented football boot technology, backed by the R&D Tax Incentive

The situation
Developed on the back of an Australian invention, Concave Football Boots has refined a product that has been patented globally, aiming to be the most technologically advanced football boot in the world, offering players more power, better control, improved metatarsal protection and greater kicking accuracy.
What GrantReady did
With control and registration of the IP involved, Concave was in an ideal position to benefit from the R&D Tax Incentive. GrantReady supported the company in accessing the incentive for its Australian research activities.
Outcome
Globally patented boot technology, positioned to benefit from the R&D Tax Incentive
