Our programs

Two programs. One specialist team.

GrantReady manages the R&D Tax Incentive and Export Market Development Grant (EMDG) claims process from eligibility assessment through lodgement and payment.

R&D Tax Incentive

Get back up to 43.5 cents for every dollar of eligible R&D expenditure

  • Up to 43.5% offset for companies under $20M turnover
  • Refundable, even loss-making companies receive cash
  • Retrospective, claim within 10 months after end of your income year
  • End-to-end claim managed by R&D specialists
43.5%Maximum offset (indicative) on eligible R&D spend
EMDG

Recover up to 50% of your overseas marketing expenditure

The Export Market Development Grant reimburses eligible export promotional expenditure for businesses with annual income under $20M. Trade shows, overseas travel, IP registration, and more.

  • Up to 50% of eligible export spend reimbursed
  • Available to exporters with income under $20M
  • Grant paid directly to your bank account
  • Covers trade shows, travel, IP registration
